Daejeon, Korea

I am a PhD student at KAIST EE, working at Multimodal AI Lab under the supervision of Prof. Joon Son Chung. I received my MS from KAIST in February 2025 and continued as a PhD student in the same lab right after. [Academic CV]

My research focuses on efficient speech generation, spanning model architecture, inference optimization, and post-training — with particular interest in LLM-based speech models. I'm open to academic collaborations — feel free to reach out at tandat@mmai.io.

In 2022, I graduated from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Ho Chi Minh University of Technology – VNU, advised by Dr. Duc Dung Nguyen at ML4U-AITech Lab.
